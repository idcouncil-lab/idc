About Us

Reviving Iraq's Authentic Palm Heritage

The Iraqi Dates Council is a specialized authority established to develop and organize the dates industry in Iraq. We work to connect farmers, producers, and exporters in an integrated system aimed at improving production quality and promoting Iraqi dates in global markets.

We believe that Iraqi palms are a national treasure worthy of protection and development. We strive to raise awareness about the importance of this strategic crop and its role in the national economy.
